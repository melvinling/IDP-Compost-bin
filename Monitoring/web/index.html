About this system

Six measurements: distance, EC, pH, CO₂, moisture and temperature. The configurable four-parameter soil profile uses registers 2, 3, 0 and 1 at ID 1 / 4800 baud. CO₂ uses ID 1 / 9600 baud. Actual sensor replies and bus compatibility need hardware verification.

Samples start every four seconds. Up to 900 samples stay in RAM. All six inputs must be valid and younger than 12 seconds for automatic operation. Display pause does not stop acquisition or control. Manual requests last five seconds; automatic pulses: water 2 seconds, dry 8 seconds, mix 5 seconds. Cooldowns: pump and motor 60 seconds, fan 30 seconds. Fan and pump are mutually exclusive.

Boot defaults to manual / sealed fermentation, with actuators OFF. Select finishing explicitly to operate them. Stop All latches outputs OFF until reset. Individual stops block that automatic channel until the mode is reselected. Continuous operation is limited to ten seconds.

The Random Forest was trained on 1,200 artificial examples. Its confidence describes the synthetic model, and its thresholds are demonstration settings. Physical outputs remain disabled until pins, polarity and hardware behavior are verified.
